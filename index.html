The short version: Cosmic Easy Phone has no ads, subscriptions, accounts, analytics, or tracking. The app does not sell personal information. Its tools work on your device, and pictures taken with its camera stay on your phone.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cosmic Easy Phone Android application (the “App”), provided by Frogdadday (the “Developer”).

Our promise

Cosmic Easy Phone is free to use. It contains no advertisements, subscriptions, paywalls, paid features, user accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, profiling, or user tracking. The Developer does not collect, receive, sell, rent, or share personal information through the App.

The released App does not use a Developer-operated server or cloud-sync service. It does not upload camera pictures, contacts, messages, ringtone choices, stopwatch information, or settings activity to the Developer.

Information used and stored on your device

Camera pictures and interval sessions

The easy camera uses the phone’s camera only while its camera screen is open. Camera audio is disabled, and the App does not record sound. Pictures are processed locally and saved in the shared Pictures/Cosmic Easy Phone album so they can appear in gallery apps. The Developer cannot see or retrieve them.

During a timer or interval session, the App temporarily keeps the number of pictures, countdown, progress, and references to pictures from that session in memory. This session information is not uploaded. Leaving or interrupting the camera stops an active session safely. Saved pictures remain on the phone.

The review screen reads only pictures taken during the current camera session. If you select Make this my background, Android reads the selected saved picture and applies it to the Home screen, Lock screen, or both, as you choose.

Stopwatch recovery

To recover the stopwatch after Android closes or restarts the App, Cosmic Easy Phone stores the elapsed time, whether the stopwatch was running, and the time it was last saved. This small record stays in the App’s private on-device storage. It does not contain a name, account, or activity history.

Other phone tools

Most other tools open a specific Android setting or another app chosen by the phone. Cosmic Easy Phone does not read the contents of your contacts or messages. It does not read which settings you change. When it opens Contacts, Messages, Clock, system Settings, the phone camera fallback, a wallpaper chooser, or another Android screen, that separate app or service controls what happens there.

Android permissions and special access

Cosmic Easy Phone requests only access needed for features the user chooses:

  • Camera: shows the camera preview and takes pictures. The permission is requested when the easy camera is first opened.
  • Flash: uses available camera-flash hardware when you turn Flash on, and powers the separate flashlight tool while that tool is active.
  • Photos or storage on Android 9 and earlier: allows newly taken pictures to be saved in the public Cosmic Easy Phone album. Newer Android versions do not require broad storage access for this.
  • Change system settings: Android may ask you to allow this special access before the App can apply a ringtone you select. The App uses it for that ringtone action.
  • Set wallpaper: lets the App apply a camera picture as a background after you choose the picture and destination.
  • Set alarms and timers: sends the time you chose to an installed Clock app, which shows its own confirmation screen.
  • Keep screen awake: keeps the display on only while a camera timer or interval session is running. The setting is released when the session finishes, stops, or is interrupted.

If a phone denies access or does not support a destination, the App explains what happened and may offer the closest Android screen. Denying an optional permission does not give the Developer any information.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, profiling, social-media, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not use cookies or fingerprinting and does not create a user profile.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Camera pictures remain in the phone’s shared Cosmic Easy Phone album until you delete them using a gallery or file app. Because they are saved outside the App’s private storage, uninstalling Cosmic Easy Phone may not delete those pictures.

The private stopwatch-recovery record is removed when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Android backup is disabled for Cosmic Easy Phone, so the App does not ask Android to back up its private data. The Developer has no remote copy and cannot recover or delete information stored on your phone.

Other apps, Google Play, and this policy page

Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, and apps opened at your request may independently process information needed to provide their services. Their practices are governed by their own privacy policies and settings. Cosmic Easy Phone does not control those services.

This policy webpage is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it happens in your browser and is separate from using the App. GitHub may process connection and usage information under its privacy statement. This page contains no added analytics, advertisements, cookies, forms, or tracking scripts.

Children’s privacy

The App does not transmit personal information to the Developer from any user, including children. It has no account, chat, public profile, or in-app sharing service.

Please do not send private pictures or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your question.
